1. Introduction & Overview

UK Secure Browser ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a high-speed, secure mobile web browser integrated with private United Kingdom (UK) VPN routing. We believe privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict zero-logging practices, data handling, and third-party SDK integrations.

🔒 Zero-Log Commitment

We do NOT track, collect, sell, or profile your browsing activity. Your web requests, searches, visited URLs, and IP destinations remain exclusively on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Remote Browsing History: URLs visited, page titles, download history, and search queries are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room/SQLite database and are never sent to our servers.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your real name, phone number, or email address to use the browser.
  • No Traffic Packet Logging: We do not inspect, log, intercept, or sell packet payloads, DNS inquiries, or communication metadata.

3. Android VpnService Declaration & Disclosure

In accordance with Google Play's VpnService Policy, UK Secure Browser declares the following:

  • Purpose of VpnService: The app utilizes Android's official android.net.VpnService API strictly to establish an encrypted device-level tunnel to sovereign United Kingdom network nodes.
  • User-Initiated: The VPN tunnel only activates when the user explicitly taps the "Connect" button. Connection state, active server location, and encrypted session duration are visibly indicated in the app and the Android system notification bar.
  • Encryption Standards: All tunneled traffic is protected with industry-standard cryptographic protocols.
  • No Traffic Interception: The VPN tunnel does not modify web content, inject unauthorized advertisements, or harvest personal credentials.

4. Local Storage & Device Data Controls

All user configurations and navigation states are managed with full user autonomy:

  • One-Tap History & Cache Erasure: You can permanently wipe all cached cookies, local storage, browsing records, and web cache via the in-app Privacy Dashboard ("Clear Browsing Data").
  • Android KeyStore Encryption: Internal tokens and server endpoints are protected using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption via the Android KeyStore system.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

To provide high-speed bandwidth without charging subscription fees, the application integrates the Unity Ads SDK (com.unity3d.ads:unity-ads):

  • Ad Formats: Banner ads, interstitial transitions, and user-initiated rewarded video ads.
  • Telemetry: Unity Ads may collect technical device identifiers (such as OS version, device model, and approximate regional carrier) to serve relevant advertisements, in compliance with Unity's Privacy Policy.
  • VIP Turbo Boost: Users can voluntarily watch rewarded video ads to earn 30 minutes of high-speed VIP tunnel bandwidth.

6. Children's Privacy

UK Secure Browser is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors.
